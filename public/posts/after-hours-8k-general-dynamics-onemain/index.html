Companies frequently file Form 8-K material-event disclosures right after the market closes, once the day’s trading is done. Two from Friday afternoon worth a plain-language read.

General Dynamics: a new board member, filed under Item 5.02

General Dynamics Corporation filed an 8-K disclosing that its board elected Danny Deep as a new director, effective August 5, 2026. Per the filing, Deep currently serves as the company’s president and won’t receive additional compensation for the board seat. The filing states there’s no arrangement or understanding tied to the appointment, and that Deep hasn’t yet been assigned to a board committee. A press release announcing the appointment is attached as an exhibit.

This falls under Item 5.02 — the item companies use for director and officer appointments, departures, and related compensatory arrangements. It’s one of the more common reasons a company files an 8-K outside of earnings season.

OneMain Holdings: $600 million in new senior notes, filed under Item 1.01

OneMain Holdings, Inc. filed an 8-K disclosing an underwriting agreement, entered August 6, 2026, for its subsidiary OneMain Finance Corporation to issue $600.0 million in aggregate principal amount of 7.125% Senior Notes due 2034. Barclays Capital and BMO Capital Markets are named as representatives of the underwriters. The filing states the offering is expected to close August 20, 2026, and that proceeds are intended for general corporate purposes, which may include debt repurchases or repayments.

This is filed under Item 1.01, the item for material definitive agreements — covering things like financing arrangements, major contracts, and debt issuances.

Why “after the bell” matters

Both filings landed in the last hour of the trading day (SEC filing timestamps put them between 5:00 and 5:31pm Eastern) — a routine pattern, since companies often prefer to disclose material events once the market has stopped trading for the day rather than mid-session. That timing is worth knowing about, but it isn’t itself a signal about where either stock goes next; this recap describes what was filed, nothing more.
